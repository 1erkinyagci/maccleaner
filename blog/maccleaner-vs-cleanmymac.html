MacCleaner vs CleanMyMac: Which Fits You? (2026)

MacCleaner · 2026-07-20 · 6 min read

Short answer

CleanMyMac (by MacPaw) is a broad, paid all-in-one suite — cleanup plus malware scanning, an app uninstaller, maintenance and performance tools. MacCleaner is deliberately narrow: focused, transparent storage cleanup with a free tier, read-only scans and review-before-delete. Choose CleanMyMac if you want one app for everything; choose MacCleaner if you want focused, honest space recovery without the suite or the subscription.

Two different philosophies

This isn't really a feature-for-feature race — the two tools are built for different jobs. CleanMyMac wants to be the one app that maintains your whole Mac. MacCleaner wants to do one thing — reclaim disk space — as transparently as possible. Picking well means picking the philosophy that fits you.

Where CleanMyMac fits

If you want a single paid app that also scans for malware, uninstalls apps cleanly, runs maintenance scripts and offers performance tune-ups, CleanMyMac is the established choice and does far more than clean storage. The costs are a subscription, a larger footprint, and more features than you need if space is your only concern.

MacPaw updates CleanMyMac's features and pricing regularly — check their official site for the current details before deciding.

Where MacCleaner fits

MacCleaner does one job well: Scan → Preview → Clean. Read-only, local-first scans; every group opens to the exact files and measured sizes; rebuildable items are marked safe and personal ones are never pre-selected; you choose Trash (recoverable) or permanent. It's free for up to 500 MB of cleanup a day, signed and notarized, with no ads, tracking or scareware. It does not scan for malware or uninstall apps — by design.

Frequently asked questions

Is MacCleaner a CleanMyMac alternative?

For storage cleanup, yes — MacCleaner is a focused, trust-first alternative with a free tier. It doesn't replace CleanMyMac's malware scanning or uninstaller, which are separate features of that suite.

What's the difference between MacCleaner and CleanMyMac?

CleanMyMac is an all-in-one paid maintenance suite; MacCleaner is a focused storage cleaner with read-only scans, review-before-delete and a free tier. Different scope, different price model.

Is CleanMyMac worth it?

If you want one app for malware, uninstalling and maintenance as well as cleanup, it can be. If you only want to reclaim disk space transparently, a focused free-tier tool may be a better fit. Check current pricing on MacPaw's site.
