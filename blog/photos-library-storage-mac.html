Photos Library Taking Up Space on a Mac: What to Do

MacCleaner · 2026-08-04 · 6 min read

Short answer

The Photos library is usually the single largest personal item on a Mac. The honest fixes: turn on iCloud Photos with Optimise Mac Storage (full-res in the cloud, lighter copies locally), move the library to an external SSD, and remove duplicates. Don't delete inside the .photoslibrary package by hand — manage it through Photos. Photo caches can be cleared separately.

Why it's the biggest thing on your Mac

Years of high-res photos and 4K video add up fast — a Photos library of 50–200GB is common. It's personal data, so the goal isn't to delete it, it's to store it smarter.

The right ways to shrink its footprint

  • iCloud Photos + Optimise Mac Storage (Photos → Settings → iCloud) — keeps originals in the cloud and lightweight versions locally. The biggest, safest win.
  • Move the library to an external SSD — hold Option when opening Photos to pick its location.
  • Remove duplicates — Photos → Duplicates album merges them.
Never delete files inside the .photoslibrary package in Finder — you can corrupt the library. Always manage photos through the Photos app.

The part a cleaner can safely help with

Frequently asked questions

Why is my Photos library so big on my Mac?

High-res photos and 4K video accumulate over years, easily reaching 50–200GB. Use iCloud Photos with Optimise Mac Storage or move the library to an external SSD to shrink the local footprint.

Can I delete files inside the Photos library to save space?

No — never edit the .photoslibrary package in Finder; it can corrupt your library. Manage photos through the Photos app, and use iCloud/offloading to reduce the local size.

How do I move my Photos library to an external drive?

Quit Photos, copy the .photoslibrary to the external SSD, then hold Option while opening Photos and choose the library in its new location.
