How to Wipe & Clean a MacBook Before Selling (2026)

MacCleaner · 2026-08-04 · 6 min read

Short answer

Before selling a MacBook, sign out of your accounts (iCloud/Find My, iMessage, Music), back up anything you want to keep, then use Erase All Content and Settings for a clean, secure handoff. That is the real wipe for a sale — a cleaner app is not a substitute. If instead you're keeping the Mac and just clearing it out, remove caches, downloads and developer junk first.

The safe pre-sale checklist

  1. Back up anything you want to keep (Time Machine or an external drive).
  2. Sign out of iCloud/Find My, iMessage and Music/TV so the new owner isn't locked out by Activation Lock.
  3. Erase All Content and Settings — System Settings → General → Transfer or Reset. This securely wipes your data and gives a factory-fresh Mac.
For a sale, Erase All Content and Settings is the step that matters — it removes your data and deactivates the Mac from your account. Don't rely on manually deleting files.

If you're clearing space, not selling

Keeping the Mac but want it clean and fast on space? Clear the rebuildable clutter: app caches, old Downloads, and — on a dev Mac — Xcode and node_modules. Then empty the Trash to commit the space.

Where MacCleaner fits (and doesn't)

Being honest: MacCleaner is a storage tool, not a secure-erase utility. Use it to see and clear recoverable clutter before you hand the Mac over or reset it — but for an actual sale, always finish with Erase All Content and Settings. That combination gives you a clean drive and a safe handoff.

Frequently asked questions

How do I fully wipe a MacBook before selling?

Sign out of iCloud/Find My, iMessage and Music, back up your data, then use System Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Erase All Content and Settings. That securely erases everything and deactivates the Mac.

Does MacCleaner erase a Mac for selling?

No — it's a storage cleaner, not a secure-wipe tool. Use Apple's Erase All Content and Settings for a sale. MacCleaner is for clearing recoverable clutter while you keep the Mac.

Do I need to reinstall macOS before selling?

Usually not. Erase All Content and Settings leaves a clean macOS install ready for the new owner's setup. A full reinstall is only needed if something goes wrong.
