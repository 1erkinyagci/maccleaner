Mac Mini Storage in 2026: How Much Do You Need?

MacCleaner · 2026-08-05 · 5 min read

Short answer

For a Mac mini, buy for what you can't change later: the SSD isn't upgradeable, but the mini's desktop form makes external drives easy and cheap. Budget 20–30GB for macOS and Apple Intelligence, then size for your work — base tiers suit browsing and office work, while video and development need more or a fast external SSD.

Buy internal for what must be fast

The internal SSD is the fastest storage you'll have and the one you can't upgrade. Keep the OS, apps and active projects there. Everything archival — finished video, photo libraries, backups — can live on an external drive, which is far cheaper per gigabyte.

How much macOS takes first

Expect 20–30GB gone before your files: macOS, recovery and the on-device Apple Intelligence models. Keep another 10–15% of the drive free so macOS has swap headroom.

Desktop advantage: unlike a laptop, a permanently attached external SSD on a mini is genuinely practical — so a smaller internal tier is a reasonable trade.

Frequently asked questions

How much storage do I need on a Mac mini?

Budget 20–30GB for macOS and Apple Intelligence, keep 10–15% free, and size the rest for your work. Because a mini sits on a desk, a fast external SSD is a practical way to add cheap capacity.

Can you upgrade Mac mini storage?

Not the internal SSD — it's not user-upgradeable. External SSDs over Thunderbolt are the practical expansion route.

Is an external SSD good enough for a Mac mini?

Yes, for libraries, archives and even active project files over Thunderbolt. Keep the OS and apps on the internal drive for speed.
