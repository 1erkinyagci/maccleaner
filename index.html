/ 06 — faq

Good to know

Is MacCleaner safe?

Yes. It never deletes anything automatically, moves files to Trash by default, and protects system-critical and personal paths — those can't even be selected.

Does it delete files automatically?

No. Every cleanup is analyze → review → confirm. Nothing is removed without your approval.

Who is Scout?

Scout is your built-in AI helper. It narrates the scan, explains what each group is and why it's safe to recover, and answers your questions about your Mac's storage — always honest, never pushy.

Is this a RAM cleaner?

No. MacCleaner frees disk space — it makes no false performance or "memory boost" claims.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning is local-first and runs entirely on your Mac.

What can I do for free?

Unlimited read-only scans, Scout's full AI help, and up to 500 MB of cleanup per day. Pro removes the daily cap for unlimited cleanup.

Why is System Data so large on my Mac?

It's mostly caches, logs, dev junk, and leftovers — exactly what the System Data Inspector helps you understand and clear.

Can I restore cleaned files?

Yes. Cleanups land in MacCleaner's recoverable Trash first — restore any item until you choose to empty it.

Where can I learn more about Mac storage?

Our blog has practical guides: what System Data actually is, clearing caches safely, and reclaiming Xcode's disk space.
